# The audience includes teams building automated tooling and AI agents

The learners are people who need ASPICE quickly and whose work involves automated engineering tooling and AI agents. [MISSION.md](../MISSION.md) now says so, and lessons 10–13 cover what traceability checkers, stage gates and AI-generated work products can evidence. The misconceptions such teams are prone to are the ones lesson 13 and the misconceptions sheet target: cybersecurity counted as part of 4.1, traceability placed at level 2, gates mapped to "V-model phases", SYS.1 mistaken for system requirements, verification independence credited to ASPICE, and green checks treated as proof of compliance.

**Implications**: new lessons stay generic and use the Seat-Heat team; no real company, product or tool is named.
