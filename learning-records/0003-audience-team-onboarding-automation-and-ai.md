# The audience is a team getting up to speed, including on automation and AI

Hugo is the author; the learners are people at a company who need ASPICE quickly, and whose work involves automated engineering tooling and AI agents. [MISSION.md](../MISSION.md) now says so, and lessons 10–13 cover what traceability checkers, stage gates and AI-generated work products can evidence. The company's internal material showed the misconceptions such a team is prone to: cybersecurity counted as part of 4.1, traceability placed at level 2, gates mapped to "V-model phases", SYS.1 mistaken for system requirements, verification independence credited to ASPICE, and green checks treated as proof. Lesson 13 and the misconceptions sheet target exactly these.

**Implications**: the company, its roles and its product must never appear in the course. New lessons stay generic and use the Seat-Heat team.
