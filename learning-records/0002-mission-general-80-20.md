# The mission became general, and the whole course is wanted at once

After lesson 1, Hugo asked for the course to stop being about rig and to be finished in full, built on the 80/20 principle: the shortest route to being well versed in ASPICE. [MISSION.md](../MISSION.md) was rewritten to match. With no chance to pace lessons one by one, spacing and interleaving are built into the lessons themselves (a warm-up in each, an interleaved capstone). All examples moved to one invented project, the Seat-Heat team, so they reinforce each other.

**Implications**: a later session should find out which lessons actually stuck, via the capstone results or a quick quiz, before adding anything. Learning records for individual concepts wait for that evidence.
