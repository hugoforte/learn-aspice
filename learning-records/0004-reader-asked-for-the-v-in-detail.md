# A reader asked for the V in detail

A reader from the target audience said the course talks about traceability but never opens the processes of the V: SYS.1–SYS.5, SWE.1–SWE.6 and VAL.1. For their teams those processes are the core of the work, including work done by coding agents. Lessons 14–16 answer that, as a detour that fits after lesson 4. They are appended rather than inserted so that published links and Anki card IDs stay stable.

**Implications**: the V lessons stay at the level of what each process asks for and the rules that decide ratings, not HWE or MLE detail. VAL.1 is taught mainly to show where it does not apply, since the Guidelines exclude ECUs and embedded software from it. This is a request, not evidence of what stuck; check what learners retain before extending the part further.
