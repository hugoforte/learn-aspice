# Notes

- The whole course was requested at once, built on the 80/20 principle: the shortest path to being well versed. Lessons therefore carry their own spacing: most open with a warm-up retrieving earlier lessons, and the capstone and the red-pen lesson interleave everything.
- Examples use one running fictional project, **the Seat-Heat team**: eight people at a Tier-1 supplier building seat-heating control software for an OEM, working in Jira, Git, pull requests and CI. Keep every example inside that project so later lessons can reuse it.
- Learners include teams that build or use automated engineering tooling and AI agents; lessons 10–13 serve them. Keep those lessons generic: no real company, product or tool names.
- The workspace lives at `D:\learn\aspice` and is published at <https://hugoforte.github.io/learn-aspice/> (repo `hugoforte/learn-aspice`, Pages from `main`).
- Neither the PAM nor the Guidelines may be redistributed: link with `#page=N`, quote only short phrases. PAM PDF pages equal printed pages; the Guidelines PDF page is the printed page + 2.
- `assets/quiz.js` shuffles answer buttons except ordered scales (N/P/L/F, digits, a/b/c). Still balance keys by content: mix Yes/No, Right/Wrong, Credits/Ignores and N/P/L/F answers.
- Open lessons with `Start-Process <path>` from PowerShell.
