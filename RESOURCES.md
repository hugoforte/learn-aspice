# Automotive SPICE Resources

## Knowledge

- [VDA QMC: _Automotive SPICE PRM/PAM 4.1 (Preview)_, 2026-04-16](https://vda-qmc.de/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Automotive-SPICE-PAM-4.1_Preview.pdf)
  The model itself. Chapter 3 (pp. 13–27) is the whole model in miniature; chapter 4 holds each process's purpose, outcomes and base practices; chapter 5 the process attributes and generic practices; Annex B the information items. Use for: every claim about what the model says. `#page=N` equals the printed page.
- [VDA QMC: _Automotive SPICE Guidelines_, 3rd revised edition for PAM 4.1, March 2026](https://vda-qmc.de/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/ASPICE_BGB_2.1_Yellow-Volume_final-2.pdf)
  How assessors interpret and rate the model: the recommended VDA Scope, sampling, aggregation, and hundreds of rating rules (`[TAC.RL.1]` and so on). Part 1 (pp. 26–44) and chapters 4–5 (level 2 and 3) are the high-yield parts. Use for: anything about how a rating is actually reached. `#page=N` is the printed page + 2.
- [VDA QMC: Automotive SPICE publications page](https://vda-qmc.de/en/automotive-spice/automotive-spice-veroeffentlichungen/)
  The only sanctioned download point for the PAM and its companions. Use for: checking which version is current.
- [VDA QMC: _Automotive SPICE PAM 4.0_ (2023)](https://vda-qmc.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Automotive-SPICE-PAM-v40.pdf)
  The previous release, which most training material still targets. Use for: comparing when a secondary source quotes 4.0.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [intacs: training centre and Gate4SPICE events](https://intacs.info/training-center)
  The assessor certification body runs regular, often free, online and onsite "Gate4SPICE" talks where practising assessors discuss the model and its changes. Use for: testing your reading of a rating against people who rate for a living.

## Gaps

- No free, high-trust source of full worked assessment reports; the capstone's dossier is invented for that reason.
